About

I'm Taranveer Singh Gill, a Data Engineer who enjoys building things that make data useful. I like working on scalable pipelines, cloud technologies, and data platforms that help turn raw information into something people can actually use to make better decisions.

In my current role at DEEPDATA INC, I develop Azure-based data pipelines, build ETL/ELT workflows with Databricks and PySpark, and design analytics-ready data models using Azure Synapse and Power BI. Before transitioning into data engineering, I worked as a Data Scientist in the electric vehicle industry, where I developed predictive models and dashboards to improve battery performance and support engineering decisions.

My technical interests span data engineering, cloud technologies, distributed data processing, and applied machine learning. I enjoy solving complex data challenges, optimizing pipelines, and building systems that enable organizations to make better, data-driven decisions.

I'm always looking for opportunities to learn new technologies, collaborate on impactful projects, and contribute to building reliable, scalable data solutions.
